What you are looking at

  • Observed: reservoir level, storage, inflow, release (ANA SAR); river flow (ANA gauges).
  • Modelled: reservoir inflow from rain and temperature (GR4J). Shown only where the model passed its validation test; see the validation report.
  • Derived: system storage, operating band, implied export, local inflow. Formulas are in the data-quality report.
  • Not shown, because not known: lake outline or level on each date (no bathymetry), tunnel flows, sub-daily data.
  • Display only, does not change with the date: ground imagery (Sentinel-2, dry season August 2024), land cover (ESA WorldCover 2021), lake outlines (OpenStreetMap) drawn at the terrain's own lake surface, and the sky and haze. The sun is the computed sun for the replayed date at the chosen hour.
  • White lines are catchment boundaries delineated from the terrain. Terrain is Copernicus GLO-30 at 30 m, a surface model (it includes forest canopy), for display only. Vertical scale: .
